where they located ?
nupe occupying around , square miles lowland niger basin .
they scattered over several states west-central northern nigeria , most them live niger state .
sizeable number also lives kwara kogi states as well as federal capital territory .
their major cities include bida , minna , agaie , lapai , mokwa , jebba , lafiagi , pategi .
what their lives like ?
nupe live large towns cities called ezi as well as smaller villages called tunga kangi .
they live clusters compounds forming ward .
village chief assisted village elders main authority .
nupe primarily farmers .
their main crops consist millet , guinea-corn , yams , rice , groundnuts .
there also quite few fishermen nupe villages coast .
marriages usually arranged , polygynous marriages common .
children raised their parents , grandparents , older siblings .
children respect their parents as well as share , cooperate , avoid quarrels .
what their beliefs ?
nupe primarily muslim people now .
however , few people still worship tradition spirits associated natural objects as well as some christians certain areas .
