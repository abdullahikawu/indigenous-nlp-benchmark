NUPE: Nupe, (ah kpe'u ye be Anufe, Nupenci, Nyinfe,Tapa), ezhimi eka Niger wun yi'o nyan gwata Nupe nan de tita misun Nupencizhi nyan tacin Nigeria nan. Edunfe nyan eza ezhimi ga yi Niger ah be kwara, Nasarawa toh Abuja (Federal Capital Territory).
EN: Nupe, (also called Anufe, Nupenci, Nyinfe, Tapa), is a language spoken in Niger State and is one of the major indigenous languages in Nigeria. Nupe people are also found in Niger, Kwara, Nasarawa and Abuja (Federal Capital Territory).
